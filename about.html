The Origin Story

Pioneering Frugal Autonomous Logistics in India

Higher education campuses in India face massive parcel delivery chaos daily: hundreds of courier delivery executives entering campus security perimeters, lost delivery attempts, and crowded hostel gates.

Rather than relying on foreign rovers costing upwards of ₹8–12 Lakhs that require RTK satellite positioning, our team at Nitte Meenakshi Institute of Technology (NMIT) proved that autonomous mobility can be built for ₹24,999 per unit using smart computer vision, predictive PID steering, and dual-OTP hardware security.
