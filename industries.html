Colleges & Universities

200+ Daily Gate Parcels • 20–30 min Walk Eliminated

! The Problem on Modern Higher Education Campuses

In large engineering, medical, and liberal arts universities, over 200+ courier parcels arrive at the main security gate every single day from Amazon, Flipkart, BlueDart, and Delhivery.

Security regulations rightfully prohibit external delivery riders from driving into student hostel clusters. As a result, students must walk 400–800 meters (20 to 30 minutes round-trip) multiple times a week just to retrieve small packages, missing study sessions and enduring heat or monsoon rains. Security desks become cluttered sorting dumping grounds where parcels frequently get damaged or misplaced.

✓ How CAMPUSBOT Solves This Completely

  • 1. Secure Gate Intake: The delivery courier arrives at Gate 1, authenticates with a simple 4-digit drop PIN on CampusBot's touchscreen, and places the parcel inside the cargo chamber.
  • 2. Dual-Solenoid Vault Lock: High-tensile 12V electromagnetic solenoids latch immediately, preventing unauthorized opening in transit.
  • 3. OpenCV Ground Navigation: The rover captures the paved pedestrian pathway at 15 FPS using Picamera2 and OpenCV, tracking the centroid line with predictive PID steering.
  • 4. ArUco Junction Routing: At campus bifurcations, optical markers instruct the rover whether to steer towards Boys Hostel Block A/B or Girls Hostel Station.
  • 5. Student OTP Pickup: The student receives an SMS with their unique pickup PIN, walks 30 seconds to the ground-floor hostel station, enters their PIN, and collects their parcel.

Parcel Delivery

Solving the Last 500-Meter Delivery Cost Bottleneck

! The Problem in Last-Mile Logistics

In courier supply chains (Amazon, Flipkart, BlueDart, Delhivery, DTDC), the "Last 500 Meters" accounts for over 53% of the total shipping cost. Couriers lose 15–25 minutes at every gated campus or high-density institution attempting to contact customers, wait at barriers, and locate individual apartments or hostel rooms.

Failed delivery attempts, re-delivery fuel costs, and vehicle congestion at gates make institutional delivery operations inefficient and unprofitable.

✓ How CAMPUSBOT Solves Last-Mile Courier Chaos

  • 1. 3-Minute Gate Turnaround: Delivery couriers drop off all consignments for the entire facility at a centralized gate dock in under 3 minutes, then immediately continue their delivery route.
  • 2. Automated Batch Distribution: CampusBot rovers automatically ferry parcels to designated station lockers inside the complex following pre-mapped optical pathways.
  • 3. Automated Customer Notification: Recipient receives an automated web/SMS OTP notification the moment their parcel is en route, with live countdown arrival tracking.
  • 4. Zero Redeliveries: Parcels stay safely locked in the dual-solenoid chamber until picked up with the correct one-time PIN.
