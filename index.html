About The Innovation

What is CAMPUSBOT?

CAMPUSBOT is a purpose-built autonomous mobile robot (AMR) designed specifically to solve intra-campus last-mile logistics for colleges, universities, and technology parks.

Instead of allowing hundreds of unknown courier delivery executives onto campus pathways each day, parcels are dropped off safely at the Main Security Gate.

CAMPUSBOT rovers then navigate college pathways automatically, reaching designated hostel doors with dual-lock OTP security.

01

Gate-to-Hostel Corridors

Eliminates unauthorized third-party delivery vehicles inside student residential zones. Ensures 100% perimeter security.

02

OpenCV Centroid Guidance

Real-time pathway edge tracking operating at 15 FPS with predictive PID steering. Works outdoors in bright sun and dusk.

03

Dual Solenoid OTP Cargo

High-tensile electromagnetic latching. Students receive a one-time SMS/web OTP to open their parcel compartment.
